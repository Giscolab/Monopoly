## libgsm / GSM 06.10

The modern voice-chat path fetches the classic GSM 06.10 implementation by
Jutta Degener and Carsten Bormann, Technische Universitaet Berlin, pinned to
commit `98f1708fb5e06a0dfebd58a3b40d610823db9715` from
`timothytylee/libgsm`. It is compiled with `WAV49` support to reproduce the
Microsoft WAVE_FORMAT_GSM610 framing used by the legacy ArtLib code.
